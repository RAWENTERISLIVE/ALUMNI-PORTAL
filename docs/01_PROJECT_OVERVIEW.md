# 1. Project Overview

## **Alma Connect Sphere**

**Alma Connect Sphere** is a comprehensive web portal designed to bridge the gap between Alumni, Students, and Faculty. The platform facilitates networking, mentorship, job postings, community discussions, and group events. It serves as a unified digital ecosystem to foster continuous engagement, mentorship, and professional growth.

## Core Objectives
1. **Seamless Networking**: Connect alumni with current students and faculty securely.
2. **Mentorship & Career Growth**: Facilitate mentor-mentee relationships and internal job boards.
3. **Knowledge Sharing**: Enable robust community discussions, posts, arrays of groups, and specific events.
4. **Secure Data & Access Control**: Ensure that privacy levels are maintained systematically with user roles and section-level privacy rules.

## Key Features
- **User Management & Authentication**: Standard login, registration with manual and automatic validation, dynamic JWT-based auth flows handling access and refresh tokens.
- **Dynamic Post & Feeds**: Nested comments, rich UI handling multimedia posts, bookmarking, and fine-grained feed aggregation including 'School Updates' and 'Featured Posts'.
- **Alumni Directory & Search**: Faceted search directory spanning users, cohorts, and skill bases.
- **Mentorship System**: Robust module for connecting, requesting, and scheduling mentorship sessions among registered students and willing alumni.
- **Jobs & Opportunities**: Job posting system including categorizations, remote/in-person status, tracking applications, and job-specific discussions.
- **Groups & Events**: Distinct organizational circles (Groups) combined with time-based engagements (Events) that offer RSVP features.
- **Advanced Admin Functions**: Comprehensive Super Admin and Admin dashboards for audit logs, real-time metrics, system health checks, and global moderation.
